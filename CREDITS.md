# Credits

**All visual art in Velvet Abyss is original and drawn for this game, in code.**
The four host masks and the porcelain player mask, every slot symbol, gem, item and creature,
the card deck (numerals, suit pips, face cards, back), dice, chips, interface icons, the gilded
frame and the four floor backdrops are authored as pixel grids in `src/art/` and rendered at
runtime by `src/art/engine.ts` with one 28-colour palette, one outline colour and one light
direction. Nothing is copied from another game, including the owner's earlier projects, which
were studied only for mood (the pixel crispness of Aetherforge, the gilded-ledger darkness of
The Last Wager).
